# ImmersiveAction

Turns World of Warcraft into a third-person action game: an over-the-shoulder camera that always follows the mouse, your mouse buttons cast your abilities, and talking, looting, training, shopping and questing all happen from panels near the crosshair, so the camera never has to let go.

Works in **WoW Forever** and **Retail**.

## Install

1. Download the latest **`ImmersiveAction-x.y.z.zip`** from [Releases](../../releases/latest).
2. Extract it into your AddOns folder, so you end up with an `ImmersiveAction` folder there:
   - WoW Forever: `World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns\`
   - Retail: `World of Warcraft\_retail_\Interface\AddOns\`
3. Restart the game (not just `/reload`) and make sure ImmersiveAction is ticked in the AddOns list on the character screen.

To update, do the same with the newer zip, replacing the old `ImmersiveAction` folder.

## First steps

- Type **`/ia`** for the settings. Every part of the addon is a module you can turn on or off.
- **Hold ALT** to free the cursor; **double-tap ALT** to turn ImmersiveAction off and on.
- **Left and right click** press your **1** and **2** keys, so put your main abilities there.
- **Interact** (F by default) talks, loots and uses things. ImmersiveAction turns on the game's Interact key for you.
- **T** is the quest key while ImmersiveAction is on: tap it for your next quest, hold it for your quest list. Your own T binding comes back when the addon is off.
